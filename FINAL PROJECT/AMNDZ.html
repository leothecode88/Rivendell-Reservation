About and help

Rivendell Reservations is a front-end prototype that helps travellers and the stewards of the valley book halls, chambers and gardens without double bookings.

How to reserve

  1. Browse the venues and press Reserve this venue, or use the form directly.
  2. Enter your name, email, date, time slot and number of guests.
  3. Press Confirm reservation to receive a reference number.
  4. Find, edit, complete, cancel or delete the booking under My reservations.

Rules

  • Past dates cannot be reserved.
  • A venue can hold only one active reservation per date and time slot.
  • Guests cannot exceed the venue's capacity.
  • Cancelling a reservation frees its slot for someone else.
